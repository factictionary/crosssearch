Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

VocabIQ (accessible via https://crosssearch.factictionary.in) is developed and operated by Factictionary. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information gathered while you enjoy our offline-first word puzzle and vocabulary games.

1. Information We Collect

VocabIQ is designed with a privacy-first, offline-first philosophy:

  • No Personal Account Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or provide personal credentials (like your name or phone number) to play any game mode.
  • Local Game Data: Game progress, high scores, streaks, unlocked hints, and sound preferences are stored entirely locally on your device via secure app storage.
  • Anonymous Diagnostics & Crash Data: We may collect non-identifiable technical data (device model, OS version, crash logs) to maintain performance and fix bugs.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep VocabIQ free for players worldwide, we display non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads via industry-standard advertising partners:

  • Google AdMob: Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect anonymous device identifiers and contextual telemetry to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud. For details on Google's data practices, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: If you purchase premium add-ons or ad-removal passes, transactions are processed securely by Google Play. We never see or store your financial payment details.

3. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & Family Safety)

VocabIQ contains curated, educational vocabulary content suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. All vocabulary entries, definitions, and games have been strictly filtered to remove explicit or inappropriate content.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device, you maintain complete control. Clearing app cache/data or uninstalling VocabIQ will instantly delete all locally stored data from your device.

5. Security

We value your trust in using VocabIQ. We implement commercially acceptable security standards to safeguard any minimal technical data transmitted for ad delivery or crash diagnostics.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of VocabIQ constitutes acceptance of these updates.
